# AStockGuard V3.5

V3.5 focuses on multi-period K-line analysis, local technical strategy evaluation, replay validation and tighter chart/review integration.

## Chart analysis

- DAY / WEEK / MONTH chart periods.
- Daily bars aggregate into weekly and monthly bars with correct OHLCV semantics.
- ECharts trading chart with candlesticks, volume, MA overlays, zoom/crosshair interaction and trade/plan markers.
- Chart state is isolated behind `ChartRepository` / `ChartViewModel`.

## Strategy engine

- MA5 / MA10 / MA20 trend alignment.
- MACD (DIF / DEA / histogram).
- RSI(14).
- VWAP-relative strength and volume expansion inputs.
- Structured score/action result with BUY/SELL/HOLD, reasons and buy/sell zones.

## Fund flow

- EastMoney stock and sector fund-flow repository remains provider-isolated.
- Stock main-force / super-large / large / medium / small order flow models.
- Industry/concept ranking models are available to dashboard presentation.

## Replay and review

- Historical K-line replay re-evaluates the strategy without future-bar lookahead.
- Replay statistics include return, max drawdown, win rate and trade count.
- Existing paper/replay/review dashboard cards remain separated from real holdings.

## Safety constraints

- The app remains decision-support software; it does not auto-submit real-account orders.
- Stale/mock data must not be treated as live execution evidence.
- Historical replay and technical indicators do not imply future profitability.

## Engineering

- App version: `3.5.0` (`versionCode 35`).
- CI gate: unit tests -> lint -> assembleDebug -> artifact.
- Rolling feature release continues through `latest-dev` after a successful feature-branch build.
