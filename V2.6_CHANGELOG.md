# V2.6.0 - Charts & Market Replay Data Foundation

- Added Tencent minute-line client with explicit failure fallback to Room.
- Added minute bar Room cache and migration 1 -> 2.
- Added Compose minute chart with average-price line, native candlestick chart with crosshair and signal marker, and portfolio equity curve.
- Selecting a stock in the strategy table loads its intraday and daily chart data.
- Portfolio equity curve is reconstructed from held share counts and historical closes.
