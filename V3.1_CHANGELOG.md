# V3.1 — News / Black-Swan Risk Overlay

## Added

- Configurable HTTPS RSS/Atom news sources.
- `NewsFeedClient` with RSS/Atom parsing and bounded network timeouts.
- Room `news_item` cache with database migration 5 → 6.
- `NewsRiskEngine` producing E0/E1/E2 overlay, score and evidence headlines.
- Relief/negation filtering for phrases such as lifted sanctions, ceasefire agreements and restored supply.
- Dashboard black-swan card with evidence and manual refresh.
- Background news refresh independent of the 3–60 second market loop.
- Deduplicated E2 news notifications.
- AI prompt context includes news-risk evidence while explicitly keeping news as a secondary risk overlay.
- News settings are included in non-sensitive JSON backup/restore.

## Safety / reliability rules

- News failures never stop market, R2, fund-flow or local-risk evaluation.
- Cached news is marked stale.
- News alone does not produce BUY/SELL actions.
- Only HTTPS feed URLs are accepted.
- Default refresh cadence is 15 minutes and is configurable from 5 to 120 minutes.
