# V3.3 — Paper Trading & Strategy Replay

## Added

- Persistent paper account with default 100,000 virtual cash.
- Persistent paper positions, filled orders and equity snapshots in Room.
- Database migration 7 → 8.
- Manual paper BUY/SELL in 100-share lots using the current selected quote.
- Paper account reset without touching real-position settings or actual trade logs.
- Paper equity mark-to-market on market refresh, throttled to at most one stored equity point per minute.
- One-minute intraday replay cursor with reset, step, play/pause and 5x playback.
- `ReplayEngine` with a fixed `VWAP_RECLAIM` research strategy.
- Replay metrics: return, max drawdown, closed trades, win rate, profit factor and equity curve.
- Replay trade list with explicit entry/exit reasons.
- Paper account, positions and orders included in local JSON backup/restore.

## Isolation rules

- Paper orders never call a broker or exchange API.
- Replay never mutates real positions, actual trade records or the persistent paper account.
- The default paper fee is zero rather than assuming a broker fee/tax schedule that may change; a configurable fee model can be added later.
- Replay results are historical research outputs, not future-return predictions.
